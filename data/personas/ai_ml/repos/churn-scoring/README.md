# churn-scoring

retention risk scoring: the retention team called customers at random and missed most of those about to leave.

## What it does

- Built features from 18 months of usage and billing data
- Trained gradient-boosted trees with time-based validation
- Explained each score with SHAP so agents knew why a customer was flagged

## Results

- recall at the retention team's call capacity: up 18 points over the old rules
- churn among contacted high-risk customers: down 7% in the first quarter
