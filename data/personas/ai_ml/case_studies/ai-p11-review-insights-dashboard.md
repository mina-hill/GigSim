# review insights dashboard

**What was the problem?**

The product team could not read 50,000 store reviews a month.

**How did you solve it?**

aspect-based sentiment model plus topic clustering; a dashboard that flags sudden negative spikes after releases.

**Did it work?**

Yes: reviews analysed: 50,000 per month; release regressions caught early: 2 in the first month.
