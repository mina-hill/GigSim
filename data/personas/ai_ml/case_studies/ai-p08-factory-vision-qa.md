# factory vision QA

**Context.** Inspectors missed hairline cracks on machined parts at line speed.

**What I did**

- Collected and labelled 6,000 images with the QA team
- Trained an object detector and tuned the threshold for few missed defects

**Results**

- precision: 0.93
- recall: 0.89

Tools: computer vision, object detection
