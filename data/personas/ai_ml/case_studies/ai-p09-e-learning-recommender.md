# e-learning recommender

**What was the problem?**

Learners finished one course and left without starting another.

**How did you solve it?**

implicit-feedback matrix factorisation on enrolment and completion data; a/B tested recommendations on the dashboard against "most popular".

**Did it work?**

Yes: click-through on recommendations: up 11%.
