# channel ROI study

For a ecommerce client (2024-10 to 2024-11). The team could not tell which ad channels produced paying customers.

Joined GA4 events with order data and compared last-click with position-based attribution.

Outcome: budget reallocated from low-return channels: 15%; cost per acquisition the next quarter: down 12%.
