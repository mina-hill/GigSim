# weekly revenue report automation

**What was the problem?**

The finance team spent six hours every Monday assembling the sales report in Excel.

**How did you solve it?**

star-schema model over the sales database with incremental refresh; pages for weekly revenue, top products and regional breakdowns with drill-through.

**Did it work?**

Yes: weekly reporting time: from 6 hours to 20 minutes; dashboard users: 45 managers.
