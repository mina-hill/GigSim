# deduplication project

**What was the problem?**

The CRM had duplicate contacts and broken phone numbers.

**How did you solve it?**

standardised fields and merged duplicates with fuzzy matching and a manual review list.

**Did it work?**

Yes: duplicate records removed: 18% of 30,000.
