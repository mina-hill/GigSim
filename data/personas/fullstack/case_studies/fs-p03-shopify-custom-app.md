# Shopify custom app

For a ecommerce client (2024-04 to 2024-06). Stock levels drifted between the warehouse system and the Shopify store.

Custom app syncing inventory every five minutes and on webhooks. Retry queue for failed updates with an audit log.

Outcome: overselling incidents: from about 20 a month to 1.
