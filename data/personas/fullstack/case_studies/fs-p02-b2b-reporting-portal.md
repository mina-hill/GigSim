# B2B reporting portal

For a fintech client (2024-09 to 2025-01). Each client asked for custom reports that engineers built by hand.

Self-serve dashboards with saved filters and CSV export. Tenant isolation enforced in the database with row-level security.

Outcome: custom report requests: down 70%; dashboard load time: under 1.2 s at p95.
