# ECS migration

For a fintech client (2025-06 to 2025-07). The app ran on one hand-configured server with no rollback.

Containerised services on ECS with Terraform and blue-green deploys.

Outcome: deployment rollbacks: one command, under 5 minutes; hosting cost: down 22%.
