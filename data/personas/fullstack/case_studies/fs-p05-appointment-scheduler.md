# appointment scheduler

For a healthcare client (2023-04 to 2023-08). Front-desk staff booked appointments by phone and spreadsheets.

Patient-facing booking with doctor availability and reminders. Admin calendar for staff with drag-and-drop rescheduling.

Outcome: phone bookings: down 55%; no-shows: down 18% after SMS reminders.
